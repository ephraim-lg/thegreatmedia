Questions

Google Ads in Zambia: common questions.

Do Google Ads work in Zambia?+
Yes, for products and services people actively search for. In many Zambian industries few competitors advertise on Google yet. We check the search demand for your business before recommending it.
Google Ads or Facebook ads: which is better?+
They do different jobs. Google catches people already searching for what you sell. Facebook and Instagram create demand among people who were not searching yet. Most businesses start with one and add the other once the first is profitable.
How do I pay Google from Zambia?+
With your own card, in your own Google Ads account. You own the account and the data. Our management fee is separate.
How long before Google Ads bring enquiries?+
Search ads can bring enquiries from the first week, because the people clicking are already looking. The first month is spent cutting wasted searches and improving cost per enquiry.
Do I need a website for Google Ads?+
You need a page to send people to. If your current site is slow or unclear, we build a landing page for the campaign as part of the work.
