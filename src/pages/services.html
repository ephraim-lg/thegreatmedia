About our services.

What does a digital marketing agency in Zambia do?+
It plans and runs your online advertising and the assets around it. At The Great Media that means Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google ads, websites and software, AI assistants, follow-up systems, and tracking that shows which advert produced which sale.
Which service should I start with?+
If you have no clear place to send customers, start with a website or landing page. If you have one and need more enquiries, start with Meta ads. If enquiries arrive but go unanswered, start with AI and follow-up. We recommend the right starting point when we review your application.
Do you work with businesses outside Lusaka?+
Yes. We are based in Lusaka and work with businesses across Zambia, including the Copperbelt, and with clients outside the country. Everything is run remotely with weekly reporting.
How much do your services cost?+
It depends on what your business needs, so we do not publish a price list. If your application is accepted, you get a clear proposal with a fixed fee.
