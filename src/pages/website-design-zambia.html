How much does a website cost in Zambia?+
The price depends on the number of pages, whether you need an online store or booking, and how much content we create for you. You get a fixed quote before we start.
How long does it take to build a website?+
Most business websites take two to four weeks from the day we have your content and access. Online stores and larger sites take longer.
Do you build online stores?+
Yes. We build online stores where customers can browse products, order and pay, as well as booking and registration sites for events.
Will my website show up on Google?+
We build every site with the SEO basics in place: fast loading, clear page titles, proper structure and a sitemap submitted to Google. Ranking for competitive searches takes time and content, and we will be honest about what to expect.
Can you redesign my existing website?+
Yes. We often rebuild sites that look fine but do not bring enquiries. We keep what works, fix the structure and make the next step obvious.
Do I need to pay monthly?+
No. A website is a one-off project. Your only ongoing costs are your domain and hosting, which you own.
