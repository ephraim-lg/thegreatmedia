How it works

The system, stage by stage.

Ads, pages, follow-up and tracking are components. What you're buying is the way they connect. Here's what each stage does for your business — outcome first, technology second.

STAGE 01

Attract — get in front of people who can buy

Paid campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google, built around your offer and aimed at the people most likely to become customers — not the people most likely to click. Creative and copy are tested weekly so cost per enquiry keeps dropping.

  • You get: a steady, predictable flow of enquiries you can turn up or down.
  • We handle: strategy, targeting, creative, copy, budgets, testing.
STAGE 02

Capture — turn attention into a real enquiry

A landing page or website section with one job: make the next step obvious and easy. WhatsApp, a short form or a call — whichever your customers actually use. Every enquiry lands with a name, a number and enough context to act on.

  • You get: enquiries that arrive in one place, with the details you need.
  • We handle: page design, copy, forms, WhatsApp routing, speed and mobile.
STAGE 03

Qualify — separate buyers from browsers

A few well-chosen questions before the enquiry reaches your team: what they need, when, roughly what budget. Serious buyers answer them; tyre-kickers don't. Your time goes to people who can actually pay.

  • You get: fewer wasted calls, and enquiries ranked by how serious they are.
  • We handle: the questions, the scoring, the routing rules.
STAGE 04

Follow up — fast, consistent, never forgotten

The first reply goes out within minutes, not hours. Quotes are chased on a schedule. Nothing depends on someone remembering. We build the process, the templates and the reminders — and if you have a sales team, we train them on it.

  • You get: more of the enquiries you already paid for turning into appointments.
  • We handle: follow-up sequences, WhatsApp and email templates, reminders, tracking.
STAGE 05

Convert — from appointment to paying customer

A clear path from enquiry to appointment, quote and sale. We work on the offer, the objections and the close with you, so the sales conversation moves forward instead of going quiet.

  • You get: a higher share of quotes becoming customers.
  • We handle: offer structure, sales scripts and pipeline stages; you handle the conversation.
STAGE 06

Measure — know the cost of a customer

Tracking from the ad click to the sale: cost per enquiry, cost per qualified enquiry, cost per customer, revenue attributed. Reported every week in plain language so you can see where the leak is and where to spend the next kwacha.

  • You get: a weekly report you can actually read, and decisions based on numbers.
  • We handle: pixels, conversion tracking, dashboards, reporting.
