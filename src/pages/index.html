About us

Straight answers.

What does it cost?+
It depends on what your business needs, so we do not publish a price list. If your application is accepted, we look at your numbers together and you get a clear proposal with a fixed fee and a recommended ad budget. No surprises.
Why do I have to apply?+
Because we take on only a few clients a month, and only businesses we know we can help. The application takes about 4 minutes and tells us whether you are ready: your offer, your capacity to serve more customers and your budget. If there is a fit, Ephraim messages you on WhatsApp to book a call.
How quickly can we launch?+
Within 21 working days of getting access to your accounts and content. Enquiries usually start in the first days after launch.
Do you guarantee customers?+
No, and be careful of anyone who does. We guarantee the work we control: the strategy, the build, the launch date, the weekly optimisation and the reporting. Sales also depend on your offer, your market and your follow-up.
What can AI actually do for my business?+
Reply to enquiries in seconds at any hour, ask the questions your team would ask, answer common questions about your products, and pass serious buyers to you with the details already collected.
Do you build websites and software on their own?+
Yes. We build websites, online stores, booking and payment flows and custom software as stand-alone projects. See our work for live examples.
Do you work outside Lusaka?+
Yes. We are based in Lusaka and work with businesses across Zambia and beyond. Everything runs remotely, with weekly reporting.
