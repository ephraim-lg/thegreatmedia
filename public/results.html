Case study · Beauty & hair · Zambia

How Janice Wigs sold 50+ wigs in two months from social media ads.

  • STARTING POINTA beauty brand with a good product and a small following, selling mostly to people who already knew the owner.
  • BOTTLENECKReach. The right buyers had never seen the product, and organic posts weren't finding them.
  • WHAT WE CHANGEDProduct-led Facebook and Instagram campaigns with a direct WhatsApp order path — tested and optimised every week.
  • RESULTMore than 50 wigs sold in two months, as reported by the client from ad-generated WhatsApp orders.
  • LESSONA short path from ad to order beats a beautiful feed. Every extra step between "I want it" and "I've paid" costs sales.
