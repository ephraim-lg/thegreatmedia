The problem

More enquiries ≠ more customers. (Sorry.)

  1. 01Your ads are very popular with people who will never buy. Lovely people. Terrible customers.
  2. 02An enquiry lands on WhatsApp at 9pm and gets a reply at 11am. Tomorrow. By then they've hired the guy who answered.
  3. 03Quotes go out and nobody chases them. That quote is still out there somewhere, living its best life.
  4. 04The website is pretty. It just never tells anyone what to do next — so they don't.
  5. 05Nobody knows which ad made the money, so everyone guesses. Guessing is the most expensive marketing strategy there is.

So we don't just "run ads". We build the system around them — and plug the leaks.

The system

Six stages. One machine. Zero "let's see how it goes".

You don't need to understand the tech. You need to know what each stage does for you — and that none of them are left to luck.

STAGE 01

Attract

Paid campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google, aimed at the people most likely to buy — not the ones most likely to double-tap and vanish.

 STAGE 02

Capture

Landing pages and lead forms built to do one job: turn attention into a real enquiry with a name and a number.

 STAGE 03

Qualify

A few smart questions that separate buyers from browsers before they reach you. Your time goes to people with a budget, not people with "just asking".

 STAGE 04

Follow up

Fast, consistent follow-up on WhatsApp, calls and email — with reminders, so no quote is ever left on read.

 STAGE 05

Convert

A clear path from enquiry to appointment, quote and sale — with the offer, the objections and the close all thought through.

 STAGE 06

Measure

Tracking from ad to sale, reported plainly, so you know the cost of a customer and where to spend the next kwacha.

Questions

Straight answers before you apply.

How much should we spend on ads?+
Enough to get data quickly. For most businesses with a K5,000+ sale that's K5,000–K20,000 a month to start. We'll recommend a number in the diagnostic based on your customer value and how many customers you can serve.
Is ad spend included in the fee?+
No. The fee covers strategy, build, management, follow-up systems and reporting. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta, TikTok or Google, in your own ad account, so you always own the data and can see every kwacha.
How quickly can we launch?+
Diagnostic in the first week, build in the second and third, launch by week three or four depending on how quickly we get access, content and approvals from you.
Do you guarantee customers?+
No — and run, politely, from anyone who does. We guarantee the work we control: strategy, milestones, launch, optimisation and reporting. Customers depend on your offer, market, budget and follow-up too. Our delivery commitment is above.
Do you work outside Zambia?+
Yes — the UK, the US and anywhere else one customer is worth real money. We're in Lusaka (1–2 hours ahead of London, so UK calls are easy), and pricing and ad spend are quoted in your currency.
What happens after a lead comes in?+
It's qualified, routed to the right person, and followed up within minutes — with reminders until it's booked, quoted or closed. Every step is tracked so we can see where leads stall.
Do you build landing pages and websites?+
Yes, as part of the system. Landing pages, full websites and online stores are conversion assets — we build them when the system needs them, not as a separate product.
What do you need from us?+
Access to your ad accounts and pages, a decision-maker for weekly approvals, content or product information, and someone who will follow up with leads (or the commitment to let us build that process).
How do you measure results?+
Cost per enquiry, cost per qualified enquiry, cost per customer and revenue attributed to the system — tracked from the ad click through to the sale, and reported every week in plain language.
