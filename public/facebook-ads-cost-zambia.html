How much do Facebook ads cost in Zambia?

A plain guide to what you will actually pay: the ad spend, the management fee, and how to work out the right budget for your business.

By Ephraim Banda, founder of The Great Media · Lusaka · Updated November 2026

The short answer

There is no fixed price. You choose your own budget and pay Meta directly. Most of our clients in Zambia start with K5,000 to K20,000 a month in ad spend. If you hire an agency, management is a separate fee: ours starts at K10,000 a month.

The two costs: ad spend and management

Ad spend is the money that goes to Meta (Facebook and Instagram) to show your ads. You set a daily or monthly budget and you can change or stop it at any time. It is paid with your own card from your own ad account.

Management is what you pay a person or agency to plan, create, run and improve the ads. If you run the ads yourself, this cost is your own time.

How Facebook decides what you pay

Facebook ads are sold by auction. You are charged for showing your ad to people, and the price moves with:

  • Who you target. Small, high-value audiences cost more to reach than broad ones.
  • How good the ad is. Ads that people stop for and respond to are rewarded with lower costs.
  • What you ask for. A campaign optimised for messages or leads costs more per result than one optimised for views, but the results are worth more.
  • Time of year. Costs rise when many advertisers compete, such as before holidays.

How to set your budget from your own numbers

Start from what one customer is worth, not from what feels affordable:

  1. Work out your profit from one sale. Say a windows job leaves you K8,000.
  2. Decide how much of that you would pay to win the customer. Say K1,500.
  3. Estimate how many enquiries become customers. Say 1 in 10.
  4. That means you can afford about K150 per enquiry.

If your ads bring enquiries for less than that, they are profitable and you can spend more. If they cost more, the offer, the ad or the follow-up needs fixing before the budget goes up. This is why a business selling K10,000 jobs can spend far more on ads than one selling K100 items.

What we recommend to start

For most businesses where one sale is worth K5,000 or more, we recommend K5,000 to K20,000 a month in ad spend to start. That is enough to test several audiences and ads and get clear data within weeks. Higher-value sales such as construction, real estate and vehicles usually justify more.

Boosting posts vs proper campaigns

Boosting a post is the cheapest way to start, and it is built to get likes and reach. Campaigns built in Ads Manager can be aimed at enquiries and sales, tested properly and tracked. If you have boosted posts and got likes but no customers, this is usually why.

Where the money is really wasted

In our experience the biggest waste is not the ad price. It is what happens after the click:

  • Enquiries sit on WhatsApp for hours and the buyer calls someone else.
  • Nobody follows up after sending a quote.
  • Nobody tracks which ad produced the sale, so the budget cannot move to what works.

Fixing these three things often does more than doubling the ad budget. It is how Cutrite Windows & Glass turned Facebook enquiries into K100,000+ of sales.

Questions

Quick answers

What is the minimum budget for Facebook ads in Zambia?+
Meta lets you start with a very small daily budget. In practice, very small budgets take a long time to produce useful data. We recommend at least K5,000 a month for businesses with a K5,000+ sale.
How do I pay for Facebook ads in Zambia?+
With a debit or credit card added to your own Meta ad account. You are charged by Meta directly.
Is ad spend included in an agency fee?+
With us, no. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta so you own the account and can see every kwacha. Our management fee is separate and starts at K10,000 a month.
Are Instagram ads priced differently?+
Instagram and Facebook ads are bought from the same Meta account and budget. Meta shows your ad on whichever placement gets the best result for the money.
