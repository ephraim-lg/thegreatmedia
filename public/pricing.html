Our delivery commitment

"We commit to the work we control: agreed strategy, build milestones, launch deadlines, optimisation and reporting. If we miss an agreed delivery milestone, we continue the affected work at no additional management fee until it's completed. Media spend remains the client's responsibility, and results depend on the offer, market, budget, follow-up and sales process."

Why is there a minimum of K10,000 a month?+
Because below that we can't build and run all six stages properly, and a half-built system produces half-results. If K10,000 plus ad spend isn't realistic for your business yet, we're honestly not the right fit today — and we'd rather tell you than take your money.
Why a 90-day minimum?+
Weeks one to three are diagnostic and build. Campaigns need two to four weeks of data before optimisation compounds. Judging the system in month one is judging a building by its foundations.
Can we start with just a website?+
Yes — websites and landing pages are available on their own, from K7,500, built the same way we build them inside the system (one job per page, tracked, mobile-first). Ads on their own we don't do: ads without a page and follow-up behind them waste money.
How is the fee paid?+
Monthly, in advance, by bank transfer or mobile money. Ad spend goes directly from you to the platforms and never passes through us.
