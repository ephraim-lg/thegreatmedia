How much does a website cost in Zambia?

What you are really paying for, the costs people forget, and the questions to ask before you hire anyone.

By Ephraim Banda, founder of The Great Media · Lusaka · Updated November 2026

The short answer

It depends on what the site has to do. Our own website projects start from K7,500 for a focused, conversion-built business site, and go up for larger sites, online stores and booking systems. On top of the build you pay yearly for a domain and hosting.

What drives the price

  • Number of pages. A one-page site costs less than a ten-page site.
  • Features. Online payments, a product catalogue, bookings, registration forms or member logins all add work.
  • Content. Writing the words and preparing photos takes time. If the designer does it for you, it is part of the price.
  • Custom design or template. A template is cheaper. A custom design is built around your brand and your customer.
  • Who builds it. A freelancer, an agency and a do-it-yourself builder all price differently and deliver different levels of support.

The ongoing costs people forget

  • Domain name. Your address, such as yourbusiness.com or yourbusiness.co.zm, renewed every year.
  • Hosting. Where the site lives. Paid monthly or yearly.
  • Maintenance. Updates, security and changes to content.

Ask who owns the domain and hosting accounts. They should be in your name, so you are never locked out of your own website.

Cheap website or sales tool?

Two sites can look similar and perform very differently. A site built only to "be online" tells people you exist. A site built as a sales tool loads fast on a phone, says what you do in seconds, answers the buyer's questions and makes calling or WhatsApp one tap. The second kind costs more to plan, and it is the one that brings enquiries.

Questions to ask before you hire a web designer

  1. Can I see live sites you built, on my phone?
  2. Who writes the words: you or me?
  3. Will I own the domain, the hosting and the site files?
  4. Is it fast on mobile data?
  5. Will Google be able to find it (titles, descriptions, sitemap)?
  6. Is tracking installed so I know where enquiries come from?
  7. What does it cost each year after launch?

Questions

Quick answers

Can I get a website for free?+
Free website builders exist and can work for a hobby or a very new business. They usually come with the builder's branding, limited features and an address that is not your own domain.
Should I choose .com or .co.zm?+
Either works. A .co.zm domain signals a Zambian business. A .com is more familiar internationally. Pick the one your customers will remember, and make sure it is registered in your name.
How long does a website take?+
Most business websites take two to four weeks once the content is ready. Stores and larger sites take longer.
Do I pay monthly for a website?+
Not for the build. With us it is a one-off project. Your ongoing costs are the domain and hosting.
