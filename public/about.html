The founder

Started from scratch. Judged on customers, not clicks.

I founded The Great Media in 2023 with one conviction: businesses here deserve access to the same customer acquisition thinking the best companies in the world use — not overpriced agencies that overpromise and underdeliver, and not freelancers who boost a post and disappear.

I started with no team, no inherited clients and no safety net. What I had was a deep understanding of digital advertising and an obsession with one question: did this actually produce a customer?

That question is why we stopped selling ads, websites and "strategy" as separate things. On their own, each one leaks. Connected — attract, capture, qualify, follow up, convert, measure — they become a system a business owner can rely on. That system is what we build now.

We stay deliberately lean. You talk to the person doing the work. And we screen every applicant, because taking on a business we can't help would cost you money and cost us our reputation.

How we think

Three rules we don't break.

Results over reports.

We don't celebrate impressions and reach. We celebrate enquiries, appointments and revenue. Every part of the system is built and judged on outcomes that matter to your bank account.

Honesty over hype.

We won't take your money if we don't believe we can help. We don't guarantee sales, we don't invent numbers, and every result we publish says exactly what it measures. That protects you and it protects us.

Speed over perfection.

A good system launched in three weeks beats a perfect one launched never. We move fast, measure everything, and fix the biggest bottleneck each week.
