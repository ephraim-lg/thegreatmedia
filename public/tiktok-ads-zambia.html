Questions

TikTok ads in Zambia: common questions.

Can you advertise on TikTok in Zambia?+
Yes. Zambian businesses can run paid campaigns through TikTok Ads Manager and target people in Zambia by location, age and interests.
Do I need to appear in the videos myself?+
No. Product demonstrations, customer stories, before-and-after footage and voice-over videos all work. We help you plan what to film with a phone.
Is TikTok only for young people?+
The audience is younger on average than Facebook's, though people of all ages use it. If your buyers are mostly older decision-makers, we may recommend Facebook or Google first.
Do I need lots of followers?+
No. Paid ads reach people who do not follow you. A new account can run ads from day one.
