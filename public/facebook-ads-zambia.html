Questions

Facebook ads in Zambia: common questions.

Do Facebook ads work in Zambia?+
Yes, when the offer is clear and enquiries are followed up quickly. Cutrite Windows & Glass made K100,000+ in sales from enquiries our campaigns generated, and Janice Wigs sold 50+ wigs in two months.
How much should I spend on Facebook ads?+
Enough to get clear data within weeks. The right number depends on what one customer is worth to you. Higher-value sales such as construction, real estate and vehicles usually justify more. We recommend a specific budget once we have looked at your numbers.
Can I just boost posts myself?+
You can, and for awareness it is fine. Boosting is built to get reactions. Campaigns built in Ads Manager can be optimised for enquiries and sales, tested properly and tracked to revenue.
How do I pay for Facebook ads from Zambia?+
Ad spend is paid from your own ad account, directly to Meta, with your own card. You own the account and the data, and you can see every kwacha spent. Our fee is separate.
How fast will I see results?+
Campaigns go live within 21 working days of getting access. Enquiries usually start in the first days after launch, and the first month is about finding which audience, offer and creative work best. We ask for a 90-day commitment so there is time to optimise.
Do you also run Instagram ads?+
Yes. Facebook and Instagram run from the same Meta Ads Manager, so your campaigns appear on both, as well as Messenger and WhatsApp click-to-chat ads.
