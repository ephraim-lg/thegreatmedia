Questions

About our services.

What does a digital marketing agency in Zambia do?+
It plans and runs your online advertising and the assets around it. At The Great Media that means Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google ads, websites and landing pages, WhatsApp follow-up systems, and tracking that shows which advert produced which sale.
Which service should I start with?+
If you have no clear place to send customers, start with a website or landing page. If you have one and need more enquiries, start with Facebook and Instagram ads. We recommend the right starting point in the diagnostic.
Do you work with businesses outside Lusaka?+
Yes. We are based in Lusaka and work with businesses across Zambia, including the Copperbelt, and with clients outside the country. Everything is run remotely with weekly reporting.
How much do your services cost?+
It depends on what your business needs, so we do not publish a price list. If your application is accepted, you get a clear proposal with a fixed fee.
