What an AI assistant does for you.

It does the repetitive part of sales and customer service, at any hour, so your people spend their time on customers who are ready.

  1. Instant replies, day and nightEvery enquiry gets an answer in seconds, including evenings and weekends.
  2. Lead qualificationIt asks what they need, when and where, so you know who is serious before you call.
  3. Answers to common questionsOpening hours, services, locations and how to order, answered from your own information.
  4. Hand-over to a personSerious buyers and anything unusual go to your team, with the details already collected.
  5. Follow-up that never forgetsReminders and check-ins for people who went quiet after asking.
  6. Booking and enquiry captureNames, numbers and requests recorded in one place, not lost in a chat.
  7. Trained on your businessIt uses your offers, your tone and your rules. It says when it does not know.
  8. Reports you can readHow many enquiries came in, what people asked, and how many became leads.

Speed wins the customer.

When someone messages three businesses, the first helpful reply usually gets the sale. A person cannot answer every message in seconds. An AI assistant can.

It works best next to advertising: the ads bring the enquiries, and the assistant makes sure none of them is wasted.

  1. Map. We list the questions your customers ask and what a good lead looks like.
  2. Build. We set up the assistant with your information, tone and rules.
  3. Test. You try to break it before any customer sees it.
  4. Launch. It goes live where your enquiries arrive.
  5. Improve. We review real conversations and tighten the answers.

AI for business in Zambia: common questions.

What can AI do for a small or medium business?+
Reply to enquiries in seconds, answer common questions, collect the details of what a customer needs, qualify leads, follow up with people who went quiet and pass serious buyers to your team.
Will customers know they are talking to AI?+
We recommend being open about it. Customers mostly care that they get a fast, correct answer and can reach a person when they need one.
What if the AI gives a wrong answer?+
It is set up to answer only from your information and to hand over to a person when it is unsure. We test it with you before launch and review real conversations afterwards.
Do I need new software or a new phone number?+
Usually not. We build around the channels your customers already use and tell you clearly if anything new is needed.
Does AI replace my staff?+
No. It takes the repetitive first replies off their hands so they can spend time on customers who are ready to buy.
